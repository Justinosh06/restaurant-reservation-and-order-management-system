# Privacy policy

{{RestaurantName}} ("we") respects your privacy and handles your personal data in line with Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA). This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices.

## What we collect

- **Your account:** first and last name, email address, phone number, and your password, which we store only in a scrambled (hashed) form that we can't read. If you add them in your profile, also your gender and date of birth.
- **Your bookings:** the date, time, number of guests and any notes you add (for example allergies or a birthday).
- **Your orders:** what you order at the table, when, and the dining session it belongs to.
- **Payments:** amounts, payment status and the reference Stripe gives us. **We never see or store your card number.** Card payments are handled entirely by Stripe.
- **Reviews** you choose to write.

## Why we use it

- To take and manage your bookings, seat you, and prepare your orders.
- To take payments and deposits, and to refund them.
- To email you about your bookings (confirmed, changed or cancelled) and about promotions.
- To keep records of payments, and to understand how busy the restaurant is so we can plan.

We don't sell your personal data.

## Who can see it

- **Our staff**, only as needed to serve you.
- **Stripe**, our payment provider, for card payments and refunds. Stripe handles your card details under its own privacy policy.
- An email service, to send you booking and promotion emails.
- Authorities, only where the law requires it.

## Cookies

We use only cookies that the site needs to work: to keep you signed in, to remember short-lived details between pages (such as the sign-in check), and to protect our forms. We don't use advertising cookies.

## How long we keep it

We keep your account data while your account is open. Records of payments and bookings may be kept longer where we need them for our accounts.

## Your rights

You can ask us to **see** the personal data we hold about you, **correct** it, or **delete your account**. You can update your name, phone number, gender and date of birth yourself in your profile. For anything else, contact us{{ContactSentence}}.
