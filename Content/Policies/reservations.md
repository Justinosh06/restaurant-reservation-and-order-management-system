# Reservation & deposit policy

These rules apply to every table booked online at {{RestaurantName}}.

## Booking a table

- Online bookings open **{{MinDaysAhead}} days ahead**, and up to {{DaysAhead}} days in advance. For anything sooner, please call us.
- You choose a day, an hourly time within our opening hours, and the number of guests (up to {{MaxPartySize}}). Our staff choose the table, and may seat a large group across several tables.
- Each booking holds its table for **{{SlotHours}} hours** from the booked time.
- You can't hold two bookings for the same time.

## The deposit

- Every online booking needs a **{{Deposit}} deposit**, paid by card through Stripe when you book.
- Your booking only counts once the deposit is paid. If you don't finish paying within **30 minutes**, the booking is released and nothing is charged.
- The deposit is charged in the restaurant's currency at the time you book, and stays in that currency.

## When you come

- After you pay, your booking shows as *waiting for confirmation* until our staff give it a table. You'll then see it as *confirmed*.
- Please arrive on time. Staff can check you in from 1 hour before your booking.
- Your deposit is **taken off your bill** when you pay for your meal at the table.

## Cancelling

- You can cancel online from **Reservations** in your account up to **{{CancelHours}} hours before** your booking.
- When you cancel in time, your **deposit is refunded in full** to the card you paid with. Refunds usually take **5–10 working days** to appear, depending on your bank.
- Less than {{CancelHours}} hours before your booking, it can't be cancelled online. Please call us{{PhoneSentence}}.
- If you don't come and haven't cancelled, the deposit is **not refunded**.

## If we cancel or change your booking

If we have to cancel your booking, we refund your deposit in full. If we change its date, time or party size, we let you know by email.
