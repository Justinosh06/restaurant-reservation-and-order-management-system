## 1. What is RROMS?

RROMS is the website of our restaurant. With it you can:

- look at the menu and read other diners' reviews
- book a table in advance
- order food from your phone while sitting at the table
- pay by card or at the counter
- rate the dishes you ate

Restaurant staff use the same system, through a separate staff area, to manage bookings, tables, orders and the menu.

---

## 2. Who uses it?

| Who | What they do |
|---|---|
| **Guest** (not logged in) | Looks at the menu and reviews, and orders at the table by scanning the QR code |
| **Customer** (has an account) | Everything a guest can do, plus booking tables, viewing past visits and leaving reviews |
| **Staff** | Handles orders, tables, bookings and dish availability |
| **Administrator** | Everything staff can do, plus reports, staff accounts, customer accounts and restaurant settings |

## 3. Getting started

### What you need

- A phone, tablet or computer with a web browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox)
- An email address you can open, for account login codes and booking emails

### Opening the website

Type the restaurant's website address into your browser. The restaurant will give you this address. Staff and administrators use a separate **staff address** (it starts with `admin.`).

> **Tip:** On a phone, add the website to your home screen so you can open it like an app.

## 4. Guide for customers

### 4.1 Creating an account

1. On the home page, click **Log in**, then **Create a customer account**.
2. Fill in your first name, last name, email address and phone number.
3. Choose a password. It must have:
   - at least 8 characters
   - at least one capital letter (A–Z)
   - at least one small letter (a–z)
   - at least one number (0–9)
4. Type the password again to confirm it, then click **Register**.
5. You will be taken to the login page. Log in with your new account.

> **No password? No problem.** You can also log in with just your email address. See [4.3](#43-logging-in-with-an-emailed-code).

### 4.2 Logging in with a password

1. Click **Log in**.
2. Enter your email address and click **Continue**.
3. Enter your password and click **Log in**.
4. A small box with a picture of letters and numbers (a **CAPTCHA**) appears. Type the characters you see, then confirm.
   - Can't read it? Click the refresh button to get a new picture.
   - This check stops computer programs from guessing passwords.

### 4.3 Logging in with an emailed code

Use this if you have no password, or if you have forgotten it.

1. Click **Log in**, enter your email address and click **Continue**.
2. If your account has no password, a **6-digit code** is sent to your email straight away.
   If you have a password but forgot it, click **Forgot password? Log in with a code instead**.
3. Open your email, find the message "Your login code", and type the 6 digits into the website.
4. Click **Log in**.

Things to know about the code:

- It works for **10 minutes** and can only be used **once**.
- After **5 wrong tries**, ask for a new code.
- The **Send a new code** button shows a countdown. You can ask for a new code when it reaches zero (about one minute).
- Can't find the email? Check your spam or junk folder.

> **First time?** If you log in with a code using an email that has no account yet, the website creates one for you. You will then be asked to complete your profile.

### 4.4 Completing your profile

Every customer needs a **first name, last name and phone number** on file. If any of these are missing, the website shows a **Complete your profile** page before you can continue. Fill in the details and click **Save and continue**. You will then be taken to the page you wanted.

### 4.5 Managing your profile

1. Click your name in the top bar, then **My profile**.
2. Click **Edit** to change your name, phone number, gender or date of birth.
3. Click **Save** (or **Cancel** to undo).

Your user ID and email address are shown but cannot be changed.

> **Tip:** Adding your date of birth lets you use birthday promotions.

### 4.6 Changing or setting your password

1. Open your account area and choose **Password**.
2. Click **Change password**.
3. Enter your current password (not needed if you logged in with an emailed code), then your new password twice.
4. Complete the CAPTCHA and save.

From now on, the login page will ask for your password instead of sending a code.

### 4.7 Browsing the menu and reviews

You do **not** need to log in for this.

- **Menu**: shows all dishes available today, grouped by type, with prices. Use the **search bar** to find a dish quickly; it also suggests the top-rated dishes.
- **Dish page**: click a dish to see its picture, description, star rating and customer reviews.
- **Reviews page**: shows the restaurant's overall rating and all reviews. You can show only 5-star, 4-star (and so on) reviews.
- **Home page**: shows top-rated dishes, new dishes, recent reviews and a map of the restaurant's location.

> You cannot order from the online menu. Ordering is done at the table (see [4.10](#410-ordering-at-the-table)).

### 4.8 Booking a table

You must be logged in to book.

1. Click **Book a table** (on the home page or in the menu bar).
2. Choose:
   - **Number of guests** (1 to 20)
   - **Date**: bookings open from **2 days ahead** up to **60 days ahead**. Days the restaurant is closed are greyed out.
   - **Time**: only times with enough free seats can be picked.
3. Add a note if you like (for example, a high chair or a birthday).
4. Pay the **booking deposit** on the secure card payment page (Stripe).
   - Finish paying within about 30 minutes. Your seats are held for you while you pay.
   - The deposit is taken off your bill when you dine.
5. You will get an email when your booking is received. It shows as **Pending** until staff assign you a table. It then changes to **Confirmed**, and you get another email.

**Booking statuses**

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Pending | Booking received and deposit paid; staff are choosing your table |
| Confirmed | Your table is ready for you |
| Checked in | You have arrived and been seated |
| Cancelled | The booking was cancelled |

### 4.9 Viewing or cancelling a booking

1. Go to your account and open **Reservations**.
2. Bookings are in three tabs: **Upcoming**, **Done** and **Cancelled**.
3. To cancel, click **Cancel** on an upcoming booking.
   - You can cancel online up to **24 hours** before your booking time. Your deposit is **refunded in full**.
   - Less than 24 hours before? Please phone the restaurant.

Each booking also shows its deposit payment (Paid or Refunded).

### 4.10 Ordering at the table

1. Scan the **QR code** on your table with your phone camera. The table's menu opens. No login is needed.
2. If asked, tap **Start dining session** to open your table's session.
3. Tap a dish to see its details. Choose any options (for example, size), the quantity and any special requests, then tap **Add to Cart**.
4. Open the **Cart** to check your items, change quantities or remove items.
5. Tap **Place Order**. The order goes straight to the kitchen.
6. You can order more rounds at any time during your visit.

**Following your order**

The order page updates by itself. You do not need to refresh.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| In queue | The kitchen has received your order |
| Preparing | Your food is being cooked |
| Served | Your food has been brought to the table |
| Completed | Paid for and finished |
| Cancelled | The order was cancelled |

You can cancel an order yourself only while it is still **In queue**.

> If the restaurant is outside ordering hours, the table page will tell you that ordering is closed.

### 4.11 Paying your bill

1. When all your orders have been **served**, open **Checkout** at the table.
2. Have a **promo code**? Type it in and tap **Apply**. Some codes need a minimum spend, a customer account, or a birthday in the current month.
3. Your booking deposit (if you booked) is taken off the total automatically.
4. Choose how to pay:
   - **Card (Stripe)**: you go to a secure payment page. After paying, you see a confirmation page with a link to your receipt.
   - **Pay at Counter**: go to the counter and pay. Staff confirm the payment, and your screen updates by itself. You can then download a **PDF receipt**.

### 4.12 Viewing your dining history and receipts

1. Go to your account and open **Dining history**.
2. Each box is one visit. Click it to see every order from that visit and how it was paid.
3. Click an order to see the items. For card payments, open the Stripe receipt. For counter payments, download the PDF receipt.

### 4.13 Reviewing dishes

1. In **Dining history**, open an order that has been **served** or **completed**.
2. For each dish, choose **1 to 5 stars** and, if you like, write a comment (up to 300 characters).
3. Click **Send feedback**.

Things to know:

- You keep **one review per dish**. If you rate a dish you have reviewed before, the website shows your old review. You choose to **replace** it or **keep** it.
- A review cannot be edited on the same order after it is sent.

### 4.14 Promotions and announcements

- **Promotions**: current offers are shown in a pop-up on the home page and on the **Promotions** page in your account. Customers are also emailed when a new promotion starts.
- **Notifications (bell icon)**: shows news from the restaurant. A red mark means there is something new.

### 4.15 Logging out

Click your name in the top bar, then **Log out**. Always log out on shared computers.

## 5. Guide for restaurant staff

### 5.1 Logging in

1. Go to the main website and click **Log in**.
2. Enter your staff email address and password, and complete the CAPTCHA.
3. You are moved to the **staff area** automatically.

> Staff accounts always use a password. If you forget it, ask an administrator to reset it.

Your menu on the left shows the pages you are allowed to use.

### 5.2 Orders

The **Orders** page shows live orders from every table.

1. New orders appear as **In queue**.
2. Click to move an order forward: **In queue → Preparing → Served**.
3. **Counter payments**: when a guest pays at the counter:
   - Enter the **amount received**.
   - Choose the customer's email, or type a new one (a new email gets a customer account automatically).
   - Confirm. The system shows any **change due**, and the guest's screen updates by itself.
4. The **History** tab lists finished (paid) visits by date. You can download receipts from here.

### 5.3 Table Management

This page shows the **floor plan** of the restaurant.

- **Check in guests**: the sidebar lists today's bookings that have a table. When guests arrive, click **Check in**. Check-in opens from shortly before the booking time. Made a mistake? Click **Undo**.
- **Table QR codes**: open a table and choose to show or **print its QR code** for customers to scan.
- **End dining session**: when guests leave, end the table's session. This is blocked while the table still has unfinished or unpaid orders; the system lists what needs to be finished first.
- **Add or remove tables**: choose a table size to add one. A table with guests checked in cannot be removed. If you remove a table with future bookings, those bookings go back to **Pending** so you can seat them elsewhere.

### 5.4 Reservation Management

Bookings are grouped in tabs: **Upcoming** (already have a table), **Pending** (waiting for a table), **Past** and **Cancelled**.

- **Seat a booking**: on the **Pending** tab, pick a table. The system suggests the best free table, or several tables for a large group. Seating a booking confirms it, and the customer is emailed.
- **Add a booking** (for example, a phone booking): fill in the guest's name, contact number, date, time and party size. If the time is outside opening hours, you are warned; save again to keep it anyway.
- **Change a booking**: edit the date, time or party size. The customer is emailed about the change.
- **Cancel a booking**: the booking moves to **Cancelled**, and any deposit is **refunded in full**.
- **View payment**: shows the deposit payment for a booking.

### 5.5 Menu

Staff can view the menu and switch any dish between **Available** and **Sold out**. Sold-out dishes disappear from the customer menus straight away.

### 5.6 Announcements, promotions and notifications

Staff can **read** announcements and promotions. Only administrators can create or change them. The **bell icon** shows the latest announcements.

### 5.7 Your profile

Open **Profile** to update your own name and email.

## 6. Guide for administrators

Administrators can use every staff page (see [section 5](#5-guide-for-restaurant-staff)), plus the pages below. After logging in, administrators start on the **Dashboard**.

### 6.1 Dashboard and reports

- Choose a **date range**, or click **This month**.
- See revenue, number of orders, bookings, **peak hours**, **table usage** and **top-selling dishes**, as charts.
- Each figure is compared with the previous period of the same length.
- **Export**: download a **PDF** report for printing, or a **CSV** file (opens in Excel) with the same figures plus every order.

Cancelled bookings and no-shows are left out of the booking figures.

### 6.2 Menu

Add, edit or remove dishes:

- name, description, category, price and picture
- **options** the customer chooses when ordering (for example, *Size: Regular, Large*)
- availability (Available or Sold out)

Removing a dish also removes it from carts and removes its reviews. Past orders keep the dish's name and price.

### 6.3 Staff accounts

Create staff and administrator accounts, change their role (**Staff** or **Administrator**), and reset passwords.

### 6.4 Customer accounts

Search customer accounts and delete them if needed. Administrators cannot edit customer details; customers manage those themselves.

- The customer's past orders and bookings are kept (shown as a guest), so reports do not change. Their reviews are removed.
- An account cannot be deleted while the customer is dining or has an upcoming booking. Cancel the booking first, so the deposit is refunded.

### 6.5 Announcements

Post news for customers, with a title, description, category, publish date and expiry date.

- Customers are **emailed** the first time an announcement goes live. Editing it later does not email them again.
- You can hide an announcement and show it again later.

### 6.6 Promotions

Create discount offers:

- **Discount**: a percentage, or a fixed amount
- **Minimum spend** (optional)
- **Who can use it**: everyone, members only, or birthday customers (in their birthday month)
- **Promo code**: type your own, or leave it blank to generate one
- **Start and end dates** and a poster image

Customers are emailed once when the promotion starts.

### 6.7 Restaurant Settings

Set the restaurant's name, address, phone number, currency and **weekly opening hours** (including closed days). Opening hours control which booking times customers can pick and when table ordering is open.

## 7. Frequently asked questions

**I didn't receive my login code.**
Check your spam or junk folder. Wait for the countdown on **Send a new code** to finish, then ask for a new code. Make sure the email address is spelled correctly.

**The website says my code has expired.**
Codes last 10 minutes. Click **Send a new code**.

**I typed the CAPTCHA wrongly.**
A new picture is shown each time. Click the refresh button if it is hard to read.

**Why can't I pick tomorrow for a booking?**
Bookings must be made at least 2 days ahead, so the kitchen can prepare. For a sooner booking, please phone the restaurant.

**Why is a time greyed out?**
There are not enough free seats at that time for your group, or the restaurant is closed.

**Will I get my deposit back?**
Yes, in full, if you cancel online at least 24 hours before your booking, or if the restaurant cancels it.

**Can I cancel an order?**
Yes, but only while it is still **In queue**. Once the kitchen starts preparing it, please ask a staff member.

**Why can't I pay yet?**
All of your orders must be **served** before you can check out.

**My promo code was not accepted.**
It may have expired, not be valid yet, need a minimum spend, need you to be logged in, or be a birthday code outside your birthday month.

**I'm a staff member and forgot my password.**
Ask an administrator to reset it. The emailed code is for customers only.
